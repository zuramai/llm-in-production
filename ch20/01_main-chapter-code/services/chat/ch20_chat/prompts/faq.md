# ShopFlow Customer FAQ

ShopFlow is an online shop selling electronics, home & kitchen goods, apparel, footwear,
beauty, sports, toys, books, office supplies, pet supplies, garden tools and specialty
groceries. All prices are in US dollars (USD).

## Orders

**How do I track my order?**
Once your order ships you receive an email with a tracking link. You can also see the
status under *Account → Orders*. Order statuses are: pending (payment not completed), paid,
shipped, delivered, cancelled and refunded.

**Can I change or cancel my order?**
You can cancel an order free of charge until it ships, from *Account → Orders → Cancel*.
After it ships, use a return instead. We can change the delivery address only before the
order ships; contact support as soon as possible.

**My payment failed. Was I charged?**
No. A failed payment attempt is never charged. Your bank may show a temporary authorization
hold that disappears within 1–3 business days. The order stays *pending* until a payment
succeeds; pending orders are cancelled automatically after 48 hours.

## Shipping

**How long does shipping take?**
- United States: standard 3–5 business days, express 1–2 business days.
- Canada: 5–8 business days.
- United Kingdom, Germany, Australia and Indonesia: 7–14 business days.
Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day.

**How much does shipping cost?**
Standard shipping is $5.99. It is free on orders of $75.00 or more (after discounts), and
always free for VIP members. Express shipping costs $14.99.

**Which carriers do you use?**
UPS, FedEx, USPS and DHL, depending on destination.

**My package says delivered but I don't have it.**
Check with neighbours and your building's mail room, and wait 24 hours: carriers sometimes
mark packages delivered early. If it still hasn't arrived, contact support within 14 days
of the delivery date and we will open a carrier investigation.

## Returns and refunds

**What is your return policy?**
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund. Items must be
unused and in their original packaging. Opened beauty products and grocery items cannot be
returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.

**How do I start a return?**
Go to *Account → Orders → Return items*, choose the items and a reason, and print the
prepaid label. Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items; otherwise $5.99 is
deducted from the refund.

**When will I get my refund?**
Refunds go back to your **original payment method** within **5–7 business days** after we
receive and inspect the return. Cancelled orders are refunded within 5–7 business days of
the cancellation. ShopFlow cannot refund to a different card or account.

**My item arrived damaged or defective.**
Contact support within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a free replacement or
issue a full refund, including shipping. For safety issues (overheating, smoke, swelling
batteries) stop using the product immediately and contact support; these tickets are handled
first.

## Membership

**What is VIP?**
VIP members get free standard shipping on every order and early access to sales. VIP status
is granted automatically to customers who spend $1,000.00 or more in a calendar year.

## Account and payments

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover (processed by Stripe), and PayPal. We do not
accept cryptocurrency or cash on delivery.

**I can't log in.**
Use *Forgot password* on the login page. The reset email can take up to 10 minutes; check
your spam folder. If your account is locked or you suspect someone else accessed it, contact
support and we will lock it immediately.

**How do I delete my account?**
Contact support from the email address on the account. We delete personal data within 30
days, except records we must keep for tax purposes.
