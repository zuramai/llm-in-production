# Skincare Care and Usage Guide: Face Serum, Sunscreen and Hand Cream

*Owner: Customer Care Content · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This guide covers safe use and storage of ShopFlow Beauty products, specifically face serums, sunscreens and hand creams. It also explains what to do if a product causes a reaction and how returns work for opened beauty items. Always follow the instructions printed on the product label; they take priority over this general guide.

## Before First Use: Patch Test

A patch test helps you spot a reaction before you apply a product to a larger area. This matters most for face serums, which often contain active ingredients, and for anyone with sensitive skin or known allergies.

1. Wash and dry a small area of skin, such as the inner forearm or behind the ear.
2. Apply a small amount of the product.
3. Leave it for 24 hours without covering it, and avoid washing the spot if you can.
4. If you see no redness, itching, swelling or irritation, the product is likely fine to use.

Check the ingredient list on the packaging or product page before testing. If you know you are allergic to an ingredient, do not use the product, even for a patch test.

## Using Each Product

**Face serum**
- Apply to clean skin, using a few drops. More is not better.
- Apply serum before moisturizer.
- Introduce one new active product at a time so you can identify the cause of any irritation.

**Sunscreen**
- Apply generously to exposed skin about 15 minutes before going outside, unless the label says otherwise.
- Reapply at least every 2 hours, and after swimming or heavy sweating.
- Keep it away from your eyes. If it gets in, rinse thoroughly with water.

**Hand cream**
- Apply to clean, dry hands as needed, especially after washing.
- Avoid broken or cracked skin unless the label says it is suitable.

## Storage

- Keep products in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
- Do not leave sunscreen in a hot car or in direct sun, since heat can reduce its effectiveness.
- Close caps tightly after each use and avoid touching the applicator to your skin.
- Check the label for a "period after opening" symbol and the expiry date. Stop using products that have expired or that change in smell, color or texture.

## If You Have a Reaction

Stop using the product immediately if you notice:
- Redness, rash, hives or itching
- Burning or stinging that does not quickly fade
- Swelling, especially of the face, lips or eyes

Rinse the area with lukewarm water and do not apply further product. Mild reactions often settle once you stop use, but consult a doctor or pharmacist if symptoms persist. **For severe symptoms such as difficulty breathing or significant facial swelling, seek emergency medical care right away.**

After you have taken care of your health, contact support@shopflow.example (or use chat) with your order number and the product's SKU. If possible, include a photo of the product label and the affected area. Reaction reports are logged under the `product_defect` category so our team can review them.

## Returns for Opened Items

- Opened beauty products cannot be returned, in line with our return policy, **unless they arrived damaged or defective.**
- Unopened, unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- If a product arrived damaged or defective, contact support within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping.
- Refunds go to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the return.

A reaction on its own does not make a product defective, but please contact us so we can review your case.
