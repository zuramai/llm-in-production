# Yoga Mat Care Guide

*Owner: Customer Support Content · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

Looking after your yoga mat keeps it grippy, hygienic and flat for longer. This guide covers mats sold in the Sports & Outdoors category. Materials vary (PVC, TPE, natural rubber, cork, foam), so always check the care label and your product page first. If they differ from this guide, follow the label.

## Before First Use

- Unroll the mat on a clean floor and let it lie flat for 24 hours. This removes packaging creases and curl.
- Some new mats have a slight odor or a slippery film. Wipe the surface with a damp cloth and let it air dry. The grip usually improves after a few sessions.
- If you may want to return the mat, keep the original packaging and avoid using it. See [Returns](#returns-and-defects).

## Routine Cleaning

Clean after every few sessions, or after every session if you practice hot yoga.

1. Mix a few drops of mild dish soap in a bowl of warm water, or use a cleaner made for yoga mats.
2. Wipe both sides with a soft cloth or sponge.
3. Wipe again with a cloth dampened with plain water to remove residue.
4. Lay the mat flat or hang it over a rail, away from direct sunlight, until completely dry.
5. Roll it up only when fully dry, to prevent mildew.

## Deep Cleaning

Once a month, or when the mat feels sticky or smells musty:

- Wipe with a mix of 1 part white vinegar to 3 parts water, then rinse with a damp cloth.
- Natural rubber and cork mats can be damaged by vinegar and soaking. Use only a lightly damp cloth with mild soap.
- Never submerge a mat in water for long periods.

## What to Avoid

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Machine wash or tumble dry | Heat and agitation break down the foam and surface |
| Bleach, alcohol or harsh solvents | They dry out the material and can cause cracking |
| Direct sun or a hot car | Heat causes warping, fading and stickiness |
| Oils, lotions or heavy sunscreen on the mat | They reduce grip and are hard to remove |
| Sharp objects or pet claws | Punctures and tears are not repairable |

## Storage

- Roll the mat with the top surface facing out, which helps it lie flat when unrolled.
- Store in a cool, dry place. Don't fold it or put heavy items on top.
- Keep it away from radiators and windows with strong afternoon sun.

## Fixing Common Problems

- **Slippery surface:** Wipe with a mild soap solution to remove oils, rinse and dry. Wiping with a damp cloth just before practice can help on some PVC mats.
- **Mat curls at the edges:** Lay it flat, face down, for 24 hours. A light weight on each corner can help.
- **Musty smell:** Do a deep clean, then air dry fully out of direct sun.
- **Flaking or peeling surface:** This is normal wear on some materials after heavy use. If it happens within the first few weeks, see below.
