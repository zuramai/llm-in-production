# Wireless Earbuds and Bluetooth Speakers: Care and Troubleshooting

*Owner: Customer Support · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This guide covers pairing, charging, everyday care, and what to do if your device is defective. It applies to wireless earbuds and Bluetooth speakers in our Electronics category (SKUs like SF-ELE-0073).

## Safety first

**Stop using the device immediately** if it overheats, smokes, or the battery swells (the case may not close, or the housing looks bulged). Unplug it, move it away from flammable items, and don't try to charge it again. Contact support at support@shopflow.example with a photo. These tickets are treated as urgent and handled first.

## Pairing

1. Charge the device for at least 15 minutes before first use.
2. Put it in pairing mode. For earbuds, open the case lid and hold the case button until the light flashes. For speakers, hold the Bluetooth button until you hear a tone.
3. On your phone or computer, open Bluetooth settings and select the device by name.

**If it won't pair:**
- Remove ("forget") the device in your Bluetooth settings, then pair again.
- Turn Bluetooth off and on, or restart your phone.
- Keep the device within about 3 feet during pairing and away from Wi-Fi routers and microwaves.
- Disconnect other nearby devices that may be grabbing the connection.
- For earbuds, reset them by placing both in the case and following the reset steps in the product manual. Pair them again afterward.

**If only one earbud works:** Put both earbuds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, then reopen it and let them reconnect to each other before pairing with your phone.

## Charging

- Use the cable that came in the box, or a certified USB-C or micro-USB cable, with a standard 5V adapter.
- Charge earbuds in their case. A solid or pulsing light usually means charging, and the product manual lists the exact light codes.
- Keep charging contacts clean. Wipe them with a dry cotton swab if the earbuds don't charge in the case.
- Charge at room temperature. Avoid cars, direct sun, and cold storage.
- Don't leave a device plugged in for days. Store it with roughly half a charge if you won't use it for weeks.

## Everyday care

- Wipe earbud tips and speaker grilles with a slightly damp, lint-free cloth. Dry fully before charging.
- Replace silicone tips if they're worn, or switch sizes if the fit is loose.
- Check the water-resistance (IP) rating on the product page. It does not mean the charging port or case is waterproof, and it does not cover saltwater or hot water.
- Don't use sharp tools to clean mesh or ports.
- Store earbuds in their case and speakers away from heat.
