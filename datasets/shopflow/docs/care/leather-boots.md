# Leather Boot Care Guide

*Owner: Customer Content · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

Good leather boots can last for years with a few minutes of regular care. This guide covers cleaning, conditioning, waterproofing, drying, and storage for the leather boots in our Footwear category. Always check the care label or product page first, since suede, nubuck, and coated leathers need different treatment.

## Before You Start

- **Identify your leather.** Smooth full-grain leather can take creams and polish. Suede and nubuck should only be treated with products made for them.
- **Test first.** Try any new cleaner or conditioner on a small hidden area, such as the inside of the tongue.
- **Planning a return?** Returns require items to be unused and in original packaging. Try boots on indoors, on a clean surface, and avoid wearing them outside until you've decided to keep them.

## Routine Cleaning (After Muddy or Dusty Wear)

1. Remove the laces.
2. Brush off dry dirt with a soft horsehair or similar brush, paying attention to the welt (the seam where the upper meets the sole).
3. Wipe the leather with a barely damp cloth. Use a small amount of mild leather cleaner for stubborn marks.
4. Let the boots dry fully before any further treatment.

Never use household detergents, bleach, or solvents. They strip natural oils and can crack the leather.

## Conditioning

Condition smooth leather every 1–3 months, or more often in dry climates or with heavy use.

1. Apply a thin, even layer of leather conditioner with a soft cloth.
2. Work it into creases around the ankle and toe flex points.
3. Leave it for 15–30 minutes, then buff off any excess.

Over-conditioning can soften leather too much and reduce support, so thin coats are better.

## Waterproofing

Apply a waterproofing wax or spray made for leather at the start of wet seasons, and again if water stops beading on the surface. Treat clean, dry boots only. Water-resistant treatment does not make boots fully waterproof, so avoid standing in water.

## Drying Wet Boots

- Stuff with plain paper or use boot trees to absorb moisture and hold shape.
- Dry at room temperature, away from radiators, heaters, fireplaces, and direct sun. Heat causes cracking and can weaken the adhesive that holds the sole.
- Swap damp paper after a few hours.

## Storage

- Store in a cool, dry place with airflow. Avoid sealed plastic bags.
- Use cedar boot trees or stuffing to prevent creasing.
- Before long storage, clean and condition the boots first.

## Quick Reference

| Task | How often |
|---|---|
| Brush off dirt | After each wear in dusty or muddy conditions |
| Wipe down | Weekly with regular use |
| Condition | Every 1–3 months |
| Waterproof | Seasonally or when water stops beading |

## When Something Is Wrong

Normal creasing and gradual darkening are part of leather's character. The following are not, so contact us:

- Stitching coming apart, sole separation, or cracking soon after normal wear
- Wrong item or size received
- Boots that arrived damaged

Contact support **within 30 days of delivery** with a photo. For defective or wrong items we offer a free replacement or a full refund including shipping, and return shipping is free. For other returns, $5.99 is deducted from the refund. Refunds go to your original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the return.
