# Merino Wool Care Guide: T-Shirts and Socks

*Owner: Customer Care Content · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

Merino wool is naturally odor-resistant and temperature-regulating, but it needs different care from cotton. Follow the guidance below to keep your merino t-shirts and wool socks soft and well-fitted. If the garment's care label differs from this guide, **the label takes priority**.

## Washing

- **Wash less often.** Merino resists odor, so airing a t-shirt overnight is often enough between wears. Wash after 3-5 wears, or sooner after heavy exercise.
- **Turn garments inside out** and place them in a mesh laundry bag to reduce pilling.
- **Use cold water** and a wool-safe or mild liquid detergent. Enzyme-heavy detergents can damage wool fibers.
- **Skip fabric softener.** It coats the fibers and reduces breathability and moisture-wicking.
- **Wash with similar items.** Avoid zippers, Velcro, and rough fabrics like denim, which cause pilling and snags.
- **Never use bleach.** It dissolves wool fibers.

## Drying

- **Do not tumble dry.** Heat and agitation cause shrinking and felting, which cannot be reversed.
- Press out excess water gently by rolling the item in a clean towel. Don't wring or twist.
- Dry flat on a towel away from direct sunlight and heaters. Hang socks by the toe or lay them flat.
- Reshape the neckline, cuffs, and sock heels while damp.

## Pilling and Snags

Light pilling is normal on new merino, especially under the arms and where fabric rubs against bags or seatbelts.

- Remove pills with a fabric shaver or wool comb. Don't pull them off by hand.
- If you snag a loose thread, tuck it back in with a needle or crochet hook. Don't cut it.

## Storage

- Fold t-shirts rather than hanging them, since hanging can stretch the shoulders.
- Store only clean items. Moths are attracted to body oils and food residue.
- Use breathable containers or cedar blocks for long-term storage, not sealed plastic.

## Stain Removal

Blot the stain with a clean cloth and cold water as soon as possible. Do not rub. Use a small amount of wool-safe detergent, then rinse and dry flat. Avoid hot water, which can set stains.
