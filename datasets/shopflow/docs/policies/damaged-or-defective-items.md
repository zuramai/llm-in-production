# Damaged or Defective Items

*Owner: Customer Support · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

If something you ordered from ShopFlow arrives damaged, doesn't work properly, or isn't what you ordered, we'll make it right. This page explains what to do, what we'll offer, and how safety issues are handled.

## Safety issues: act first

If a product shows any of the signs below, **stop using it immediately**:

- Overheating
- Smoke or burning smell
- A swelling or bulging battery

Unplug it, move it away from flammable materials, and stop charging it. Then contact support right away at support@shopflow.example or by chat, and include a photo if you can do so safely. Do not attempt to repair the item or ship it back before we've talked to you.

Safety reports are treated as **urgent** and handled before all other tickets. Our target is a first response within 1 hour. Support hours are listed under [Contact us](#contact-us); if you write outside those hours, we'll respond when we reopen.

## What counts as damaged or defective

- The item or its packaging was visibly broken, crushed, or leaking on arrival
- The item doesn't work as described or stops working under normal use
- You received the wrong item or the wrong variant (size, color, model)

## How to report a problem

1. **Report within 30 days of delivery.** Reports after that window may not be eligible.
2. **Take a clear photo** of the damage or defect. For shipping damage, include the outer box and shipping label if possible.
3. **Contact support** by chat or email with your order number and the SKU (for example, SF-ELE-0073) of the affected item.

Please keep the item and its packaging until we confirm next steps.

## Your options

Once we've reviewed your photo, you can choose:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Free replacement** | A new item shipped at no cost to you |
| **Full refund** | The item price **plus shipping** refunded in full |

Replacements are subject to stock availability. If the item is out of stock, we'll offer a full refund.

## Returning the item

Return shipping is **free** for defective or wrong items. Start the return under *Account → Orders → Return items*, choose your reason, and print the prepaid label. If we've told you not to send an item back (for example, a safety case), follow the instructions in our email instead.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to your **original payment method** only. ShopFlow cannot refund to a different card or account. Expect your refund within **5-7 business days** after we receive and inspect the return. Your bank may take additional time to display the credit.

## Items that arrive damaged: special cases

- **Opened beauty and grocery items** are normally not returnable, but this exception applies: if they arrived damaged or defective, we'll replace or refund them.
- **Package marked delivered but missing?** That's a different process. Wait 24 hours, then contact us within 14 days of the delivery date and we'll open a carrier investigation.
