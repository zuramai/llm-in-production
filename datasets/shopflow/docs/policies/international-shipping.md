# International Shipping

*Owner: Customer Experience · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

ShopFlow ships to five countries outside the United States. This page explains delivery times, who pays duties and taxes, and what to expect if customs holds your package.

## Countries we ship to

| Country | Delivery time (business days) |
|---|---|
| Canada | 5-8 |
| United Kingdom | 7-14 |
| Germany | 7-14 |
| Australia | 7-14 |
| Indonesia | 7-14 |

We don't currently ship to other countries. Express shipping ($14.99) is for US addresses only. International orders use standard service.

## How delivery times work

- Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.
- Delivery times start when your order ships, not when you place it.
- We use UPS, FedEx, USPS and DHL, depending on your destination. The carrier is shown in your shipping confirmation email.
- Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking link. You can also check *Account → Orders*.

Estimates are not guarantees. Customs processing, local holidays and carrier handoffs can add time, especially at the longer end of the 7-14 day range.

## Prices and currency

All prices are in US dollars (USD). Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay. If your card is issued outside the US, your bank may apply currency conversion or foreign transaction fees. We can't see or control these.

## Duties, taxes and customs fees

Orders shipped outside the US may be subject to import duties, VAT/GST and customs handling fees, depending on your country and the value and type of items.

- **These charges are the customer's responsibility.** They are set by your country's authorities and are not included in the ShopFlow order total.
- The carrier or local customs authority may ask you to pay before or at delivery. Some carriers will email or text you a payment link.
- ShopFlow does not collect these fees and can't predict the exact amount. Your local customs office or the carrier can give you an estimate.
- If you refuse to pay and the package is returned to us, the return is processed as a standard return once we receive and inspect it.

## Customs delays

Customs may hold a package for inspection, paperwork or unpaid fees. If your tracking shows "held at customs" or "clearance in progress":

1. Check your email and phone for messages from the carrier requesting payment or information.
2. Pay any outstanding fees promptly, since unpaid charges are the most common cause of long delays.
3. Allow a few extra business days for clearance.

If tracking hasn't updated for more than 5 business days after the estimated window ends, contact us and we'll ask the carrier for an update.

## Package shows delivered but you don't have it

Check with neighbors and your building's mail room, and wait 24 hours, since carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If it still hasn't arrived, contact support **within 14 days of the delivery date** and we'll open a carrier investigation.

## Returns from outside the US

The standard policy applies: 30 days from delivery, with items unused and in their original packaging. Opened beauty and grocery items can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective. Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items. Otherwise $5.99 is deducted from the refund.

Refunds go to your **original payment method** within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the return. Please contact support before sending an international return so we can provide the correct label and instructions. Duties and taxes paid to customs authorities are separate from your ShopFlow order, so contact your local customs office about reclaiming them.

If an item arrives damaged or defective, send us a photo within 30 days of delivery. We'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping. If a product shows signs of overheating, smoke or a swelling battery, stop using it immediately and contact us right away.
