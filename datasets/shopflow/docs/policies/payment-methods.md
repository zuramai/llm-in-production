# Payment Methods and Billing

*Owner: Customer Experience · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This page explains how you can pay at ShopFlow, what happens when a payment doesn't go through, and how currency and refunds work.

## Accepted payment methods

| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| Visa | Credit and debit, processed by Stripe |
| Mastercard | Credit and debit, processed by Stripe |
| American Express | Processed by Stripe |
| Discover | Processed by Stripe |
| PayPal | Pay with your PayPal balance or the funding source linked to your PayPal account |

We do **not** accept cryptocurrency or cash on delivery. ShopFlow does not sell gift cards.

## Currency

All prices are shown and charged in **US dollars (USD)**, including for international orders to Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, Australia and Indonesia. If your card or PayPal account uses another currency, your bank or PayPal performs the conversion. Exchange rates and any foreign transaction fees are set by them, not by ShopFlow.

## When you are charged

Your payment is taken when you place the order. Once it succeeds, the order moves from *pending* to *paid*. You can follow every status change under *Account → Orders*.

Shipping costs are included in the total at checkout:

- Standard: $5.99, free on orders of $75.00 or more after discounts, and always free for VIP members
- Express: $14.99

## Failed payments

**A failed payment attempt is never charged.** If your payment doesn't go through:

1. Your order stays in *pending* status.
2. You can retry with the same or a different payment method.
3. If no payment succeeds within **48 hours**, the order is cancelled automatically.

Common reasons for a decline include an incorrect card number, expiry date or security code, insufficient funds, or your bank blocking the transaction. If your details are correct, contact your bank or try PayPal.

## Authorization holds

After a failed or abandoned attempt, your bank may show a temporary **authorization hold** on your card. This is not a charge. The hold normally disappears within **1-3 business days**, depending on your bank. ShopFlow cannot release a hold early; your bank controls how quickly it clears.

If you see a hold older than 3 business days, or a charge for an order that still shows *pending*, contact support with your order number.

## Refunds

Refunds always go back to your **original payment method**. ShopFlow cannot refund to a different card or account.

- **Returns:** within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the items.
- **Cancelled orders:** within 5-7 business days of the cancellation.

Your bank or PayPal may need additional time to show the credit on your statement.

## Security

Card payments are processed by Stripe. ShopFlow does not store your full card number or security code. For PayPal, you sign in on PayPal's own page and we receive only confirmation of payment.

If you notice a charge you don't recognize or suspect someone else has used your account, contact support right away and we will lock the account immediately.
