# ShopFlow Refund Policy

*Owner: Customer Experience · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This policy explains when and how ShopFlow issues refunds, how long they take, and what is included. All amounts are in US dollars (USD).

## Refund Timing

- **Returns:** Refunds are issued within **5-7 business days** after we receive and inspect your returned items. Your order status changes to *refunded* once the return is processed.
- **Cancelled orders:** If you cancel an order after payment, you receive a full refund within **5-7 business days** of the cancellation. The order status shows *cancelled*.
- **Failed payments:** If a payment attempt fails, you are not charged. Your bank may show a temporary authorization hold that disappears within 1-3 business days. No refund is needed.

Your bank or card issuer may need additional time to show the credit on your statement.

## Refund Method

Every refund goes back to the **original payment method** used for the order, whether that was a Visa, Mastercard, American Express or Discover card (processed by Stripe) or PayPal.

ShopFlow **cannot** refund to a different card, a different account, or by another method such as cash or store credit. If your original card has expired or been replaced, contact your card issuer, as the refund is typically still routed to the same account.

## What Is Eligible

- Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery**, if unused and in their original packaging.
- Opened beauty products and grocery items cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## Return Shipping and Partial Refunds

Your refund may be reduced in these situations:

| Situation | Effect on refund |
|---|---|
| Return for any reason other than a defective or wrong item | **$5.99** return shipping is deducted |
| Defective or wrong item | No deduction; return shipping is free |
| Only some items in an order are returned | You are refunded only for the returned items |

If an order qualified for free standard shipping (orders of $75.00 or more after discounts) and you return only part of it, the refund covers the returned items only. Original shipping charges are not refunded on a standard return.

## Shipping Refunds

- **Damaged or defective items:** We refund the full amount, **including shipping**, if you choose a refund instead of a free replacement.
- **Cancelled orders:** The full order total, including shipping, is refunded.
- **Standard returns:** Original shipping is not refunded, and $5.99 return shipping is deducted.

## Damaged or Defective Items

Contact support within **30 days of delivery** and include a photo of the item. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund.

**Safety issues** such as overheating, smoke, or swelling batteries are urgent. Stop using the product immediately and contact us. These tickets are handled first.

## Cancelling an Order

You can cancel free of charge until the order ships:

1. Go to *Account → Orders*.
2. Select the order and choose *Cancel*.

If the order has already shipped, start a return once it is delivered.

## Missing Packages

If tracking says delivered but you don't have your package, wait 24 hours and check with neighbours or your building's mail room. If it still hasn't arrived, contact support within **14 days** of the delivery date and we will open a carrier investigation. If the carrier cannot locate the package, we will arrange a replacement or refund.

## How to Start a Return

1. Go to *Account → Orders → Return items*.
2. Choose the items and a reason.
3. Print the prepaid label and ship the package.
