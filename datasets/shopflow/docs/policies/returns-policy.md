# ShopFlow Returns Policy

*Owner: Customer Support · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund. This page explains what is eligible, how to start a return, and what to expect afterward.

## Eligibility at a glance

To be accepted, items must be **unused and in their original packaging**. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in your order.

| Category | Returnable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Electronics | Yes | Include all accessories, cables and manuals. |
| Home & Kitchen | Yes | Unused, in original packaging. |
| Apparel | Yes | Unworn, with tags attached. |
| Footwear | Yes | Unworn, in the original box. |
| Beauty | Unopened only | Opened items can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective. |
| Sports & Outdoors | Yes | Unused, in original packaging. |
| Toys & Games | Yes | Unused, in original packaging. |
| Books | Yes | Unmarked and undamaged. |
| Office | Yes | Unused, in original packaging. |
| Pet Supplies | Yes | Unused, in original packaging. |
| Garden | Yes | Unused, in original packaging. |
| Grocery | Unopened only | Opened items can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective. |

## How to start a return

1. Go to **Account → Orders** and select the order.
2. Choose **Return items**, select the items, and pick a reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label.
4. Pack the items securely in their original packaging and drop the parcel off with the carrier shown on the label.

Returns that are not started through your account may be delayed, so please contact support if you can't sign in.

## Return shipping costs

- **Defective or wrong items:** return shipping is free.
- **All other returns:** $5.99 is deducted from your refund to cover the prepaid label.

## Refunds

- Refunds go to your **original payment method** (the same card or PayPal account used for the order). We cannot refund to a different card or account.
- Your refund is issued within **5-7 business days** after we receive and inspect your return.
- When the refund is complete, the order status changes to *refunded*.
- Your bank may take additional time to show the credit on your statement.

## Cancelling instead of returning

If your order hasn't shipped, cancel it for free under **Account → Orders → Cancel**. Cancelled orders are fully refunded within 5-7 business days. Once an order has shipped, use a return instead.

## Exchanges

We don't process direct exchanges. To get a different size, color or item, return the original item for a refund and place a new order. To make sure the item you want is available, we recommend placing the new order right away. If your item arrived defective, we'll send a free replacement instead (see below).

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrived damaged or defective, or isn't what you ordered:

- Contact support **within 30 days of delivery** and include a **photo** of the problem.
- We'll send a **free replacement** or issue a **full refund, including shipping**, whichever you prefer.
- Damaged or defective beauty and grocery items are eligible even if opened.

**Safety issues:** if a product overheats, smokes, or has a swelling battery, stop using it immediately and contact support. These cases are handled first.

## Exceptions and special cases

- **Opened beauty and grocery items** are not returnable unless damaged or defective.
- **Items returned used, or without original packaging,** may be declined. We'll contact you about next steps.
- **Returns after 30 days** from delivery are not eligible for a refund.
- **Package marked delivered but not received?** This is not a return. Wait 24 hours, then contact support within 14 days of the delivery date and we'll open a carrier investigation.
