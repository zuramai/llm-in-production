# Price Adjustment Policy

*Owner: Customer Support · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

If the price of an item you bought drops shortly after your purchase, we will refund the difference. This page explains who qualifies, how to ask, and what is not covered.

## The short version

- If the ShopFlow price of an item falls within **7 days of your purchase**, you can request a **one-time price adjustment**.
- The adjustment is the difference between what you paid and the new price, refunded to your **original payment method**.
- We do **not** match competitor prices.

## Who is eligible

You qualify if all of the following are true:

- The item was bought on shopflow.example, and its price (in USD) dropped within 7 days of your order being placed and paid.
- Your order status is **paid**, **shipped** or **delivered**. Orders that are *pending*, *cancelled* or *refunded* are not eligible.
- You ask for the adjustment within the 7-day window.
- The item has not already received a price adjustment on this order.

The 7 days start when your payment succeeds, not when the item ships or arrives.

## How to request an adjustment

1. Contact support by chat or email at support@shopflow.example.
2. Give your order number, the item's SKU (for example, SF-ELE-0073) and the date you noticed the lower price.
3. We will check the price history for that SKU and confirm the amount.

Requests must be made within the 7-day window. Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM ET, and Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM ET. Because we count in calendar days, please contact us before the window closes if it ends on a Sunday.

## How the adjustment is paid

- The refund is the difference between your paid price and the lowest ShopFlow price during the 7-day window, after any discounts you applied.
- It goes back to the **original payment method** (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover or PayPal) within **5-7 business days** of approval.
- We cannot refund to a different card or account, and we do not issue store credit or gift cards.

## One adjustment per item

Each item on an order can be adjusted **once**. If the price drops again after an adjustment, we cannot adjust it a second time. If an order contains several different items, each one is reviewed separately.

## What is not covered

- **Competitor prices.** We do not price-match other retailers or marketplaces.
- **Shipping costs.** Standard ($5.99) and express ($14.99) fees are not adjusted. If an adjustment brings your order below $75.00, you will not be charged shipping retroactively.
- **Requests after 7 days.**
- **Items already returned or refunded.** If you return an item, the refund is based on the amount you actually paid, including any adjustment already issued.
- **Price changes caused by a different promotion code or VIP early-access pricing that you did not use.** We only compare against the public price for the SKU.

## Returns still apply

A price adjustment does not change your 30-day return window, which runs from delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging, and opened beauty and grocery items are not returnable unless damaged or defective.

## Questions

Email support@shopflow.example or start a chat from your account. We reply to emails within one business day.
