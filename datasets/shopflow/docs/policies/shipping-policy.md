# Domestic Shipping Policy

*Owner: Customer Experience · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This policy covers orders delivered within the United States. All prices are in US dollars (USD). For Canada, the UK, Germany, Australia, and Indonesia, see the international shipping section of our FAQ.

## Shipping Options and Costs

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | $5.99 |
| Standard, orders of $75.00 or more | 3-5 business days | **Free** |
| Standard, VIP members | 3-5 business days | **Free** |
| Express | 1-2 business days | $14.99 |

- The $75.00 free shipping threshold is calculated **after discounts**. If a promo code brings your total below $75.00, standard shipping is charged.
- VIP members always get free standard shipping, regardless of order size. Express shipping is still $14.99.
- Shipping is calculated at checkout and shown before you pay.

## Order Cutoff and Processing

Orders placed **before 2:00 PM Eastern Time on a business day** ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET, or on a weekend or holiday, ship the next business day.

Delivery estimates are counted in business days (Monday-Friday) after your order ships. An order ships only once payment succeeds. If a payment attempt fails, the order stays *pending* and does not ship. Pending orders are cancelled automatically after 48 hours.

Orders ship from one of our three warehouses (Edison NJ, Dallas TX, or Ontario CA). Items in a single order may ship separately, and you'll receive a tracking email for each package.

## Carriers

We ship with UPS, FedEx, USPS, and DHL, depending on destination and package. The carrier is chosen automatically and can't be selected at checkout. Your tracking email shows which carrier has your package.

## Tracking

When your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking link. You can also check the status under *Account → Orders*. Statuses are: pending, paid, shipped, delivered, cancelled, and refunded.

## P.O. Boxes

We can deliver to P.O. boxes using USPS only. UPS, FedEx, and DHL cannot deliver to P.O. boxes. Because the carrier is limited, some shipping options may not be offered for P.O. box addresses. Checkout shows the options available for your address.

## Changing Your Address or Cancelling

- **Address changes** are possible only **before your order ships**. Contact support as soon as possible. Once an order has shipped, we can't change the delivery address.
- **Cancellations** are free until the order ships. Go to *Account → Orders → Cancel*. Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
- If your order has already shipped, you can return it after delivery. See our returns policy (30 days from delivery).

## Package Shows Delivered but Isn't Here

1. Check with neighbors and your building's mail room or front desk.
2. Wait 24 hours, since carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early.
3. If it still hasn't arrived, contact support **within 14 days of the delivery date**. We'll open a carrier investigation.

## Damaged on Arrival

Contact support within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping. If a product shows signs of overheating, smoke, or a swelling battery, stop using it immediately and contact us. These cases are handled first.
