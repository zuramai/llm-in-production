# Gift Cards and Promotions

*Owner: Customer Experience · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This page explains what ShopFlow does and doesn't offer for gift cards, and how discount codes work at checkout.

## Gift cards

ShopFlow **does not sell gift cards**, whether digital or physical. If you receive a message, listing, or website claiming to sell "ShopFlow gift cards," it is not from us. Please do not pay for it, and forward it to support@shopflow.example.

Because we don't issue gift cards, they can't be used as a payment method. We accept:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover (processed by Stripe)
- PayPal

We do not accept cryptocurrency or cash on delivery.

## Discount codes

From time to time we offer promotional discount codes by email, on our site, or through partner campaigns. A typical code takes **10% off** eligible items, though the amount varies by campaign. The terms of each code are shown with the offer.

### Rules that apply to all codes

- **One code per order.** Only one discount code can be applied at checkout.
- **No stacking.** Codes can't be combined with each other. If you have several, choose the one that gives you the best saving.
- **Not applied to shipping.** Codes reduce the price of items only. Shipping charges (standard $5.99, express $14.99) are not discounted by a code.
- **Check the terms.** Some codes have an expiry date, a minimum spend, or apply only to certain categories.

### Free shipping and discounts

Free standard shipping applies to orders of **$75.00 or more after discounts**. If a code brings your item total below $75.00, standard shipping ($5.99) will be charged. VIP members always receive free standard shipping, regardless of order total or codes used.

Example: a cart of $80.00 with a 10% code is $72.00 after the discount, so standard shipping of $5.99 applies for non-VIP customers.

## Applying a code

1. Add items to your cart and go to checkout.
2. Enter the code in the **Discount code** field and select **Apply**.
3. Confirm that the discount appears in your order summary before paying.

Codes must be entered before you place the order. We can't add a code to an order after it has been placed.

## If a code doesn't work

Check that:

- The code is typed exactly as shown (codes are not case-sensitive, but spaces can cause errors).
- It hasn't expired and your cart meets any minimum spend.
- The items in your cart are eligible.
- No other code is already applied.

If it still fails, contact support with the code and a screenshot of the message you see.

## Returns on discounted orders

If you return an item bought with a discount code, your refund is the amount you actually paid for that item after the discount. Refunds go back to your **original payment method** within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the return. Our standard 30-day return policy applies.
