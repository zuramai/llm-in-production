# ShopFlow Privacy Policy

*Owner: Legal & Compliance · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This policy explains what personal information ShopFlow collects when you shop with us, how we use it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. ShopFlow is an online shop based in the United States, and all prices are in US dollars (USD).

## What We Collect

**Information you give us**
- Name, email address, and password when you create an account
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Order details, such as items, totals, and delivery preferences
- Messages you send to support by chat or email, including any photos you attach to a damaged or defective item report

**Information from payments**
- Card payments (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) are processed by Stripe, and PayPal payments are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your full card number. We keep only the order amount, payment status, and the payment method used, so we can process refunds.

**Information collected automatically**
- Device and browser type, IP address, and pages viewed, used to keep the site secure and working well
- Delivery events from our carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL), such as "shipped" and "delivered"

## How We Use Your Information

- To process, ship, and deliver your orders
- To send order confirmations, tracking links, and service messages
- To handle returns, refunds, and support requests
- To apply VIP status when you spend $1,000.00 or more in a calendar year
- To detect fraud and protect your account
- To understand how the shop is performing, using aggregated reporting

## We Do Not Sell Your Data

ShopFlow does not sell your personal information. We share it only with:
- **Payment providers** (Stripe, PayPal) to take payments and issue refunds
- **Carriers** (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL) to deliver your order. They receive your name, address, and delivery contact details.
- **Authorities**, when required by law

## How Long We Keep Your Data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account profile | Until you ask us to delete your account |
| Order and payment records | Kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes |
| Support conversations | Kept while needed to resolve your request and handle follow-up |
| Failed or abandoned checkouts | Pending orders are cancelled automatically after 48 hours |

## Deleting Your Account

You can ask us to delete your account at any time. Email us from the address registered on the account, and we will delete your personal data **within 30 days**.

We may keep records we are legally required to retain for tax purposes. These are limited to what the law requires and are not used for marketing.

## Your Choices

- **Access and correct:** You can review and update your details under *Account*.
- **Marketing emails:** Use the unsubscribe link in any promotional message. Order and security messages will still be sent.
- **Account security:** If you think someone else has accessed your account, contact support and we will lock it immediately.

## How We Protect Your Information

Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it. Our internal reporting and analytics tools use read-only access, and customer email addresses are not available to them. Payment details are handled by Stripe and PayPal under their own security standards.
