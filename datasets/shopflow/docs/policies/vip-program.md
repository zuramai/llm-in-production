# ShopFlow VIP Program

*Owner: Customer Experience · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

The ShopFlow VIP program rewards our most loyal customers with free shipping and early access to sales. There is nothing to apply for and no fee to join.

## VIP Benefits

- **Free standard shipping on every order.** There is no minimum order value. Standard shipping is normally $5.99, or free on orders of $75.00 or more after discounts.
- **Early access to sales.** VIP members can shop sale events before they open to everyone else.

Express shipping ($14.99) is not included in VIP free shipping and is charged at the regular rate.

## How to Qualify

VIP status is granted **automatically** when you spend **$1,000.00 or more in a calendar year** (January 1 to December 31). You don't need to sign up or enter a code. Once you reach the threshold, your account is upgraded to VIP and the benefits apply to your future orders.

Eligibility is based on your account's order history. To keep your spending in one place, we recommend shopping while signed in to the same account rather than checking out as a guest.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I know if I'm a VIP?**
Your account will show your membership status once you qualify. If you think you've passed $1,000.00 and don't see VIP benefits, contact support and we'll check your account.

**Do shipping savings apply to orders I've already placed?**
No. VIP benefits apply to orders placed after your account becomes VIP. Orders that were already placed are not repriced.

**Do cancelled or refunded orders count toward the $1,000.00?**
Spending is based on completed purchases. Orders that are cancelled or refunded do not count toward your total, so a large return could affect your progress toward the threshold.

**Does VIP change my return policy?**
No. The standard policy applies to everyone: returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery, items must be unused and in original packaging, and refunds go back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the return.

**Can I pay differently as a VIP?**
Payment options are the same for all customers: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover (processed by Stripe), and PayPal.

**Does VIP shipping apply to international orders?**
VIP free standard shipping applies to standard shipping on your orders. Delivery times are unchanged: 3-5 business days within the US, 5-8 to Canada, and 7-14 to the UK, Germany, Australia, and Indonesia.

## Shipping Timelines for VIP Orders

VIP orders are processed like all others. Orders placed before **2 PM Eastern Time** on a business day ship the same day from one of our warehouses in New Jersey, Texas, or California. Carriers include UPS, FedEx, USPS, and DHL, depending on destination.
