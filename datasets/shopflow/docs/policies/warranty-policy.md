# ShopFlow Limited Warranty Policy

*Owner: Customer Support · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This policy explains what ShopFlow's limited warranty covers, how long it lasts, and how to make a claim. It applies to items bought from ShopFlow and delivered to any country we ship to.

## Warranty periods

The warranty period starts on the **delivery date** shown in your order.

| Category | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Electronics | **1 year** |
| Home & Kitchen, Apparel, Footwear, Beauty, Sports & Outdoors, Toys & Games, Books, Office, Pet Supplies, Garden | **90 days** |
| Grocery | Covered only for items that arrive damaged or defective (see below) |

Grocery items are perishable, so they are handled through our damaged/defective process instead: contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photo.

## What is covered

The warranty covers **defects in materials or workmanship** that appear during normal use within the warranty period. Examples include an electronic device that stops charging, a zipper that fails on first wear, or a kitchen appliance whose motor stops working.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, drops, liquid damage, or misuse
- Normal wear and tear, including fading, scuffing, and battery capacity loss over time
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Items used outside their intended purpose or contrary to the manufacturer's instructions
- Opened beauty products, unless they arrived damaged or defective
- Loss or theft, or packages marked delivered but not received (see our Customer FAQ for missing-package steps)

## Warranty vs. our 30-day return policy

- **Within 30 days of delivery:** if an item arrived damaged or defective, report it with a photo. You can choose a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping.
- **After 30 days but within the warranty period:** the warranty applies. We will replace the item, or refund the item price if a replacement is unavailable. Original shipping charges are not refunded.

Your 30-day right to return unused items in their original packaging is separate and is not affected by this warranty.

## How to make a claim

1. **Gather your details:** order number, the item's SKU (for example, SF-ELE-0073), and the delivery date.
2. **Document the problem:** take clear photos, or a short video if the issue only shows during use.
3. **Contact support:** email support@shopflow.example or use chat. Hours are Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM ET and Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM ET. Describe what happened and when you first noticed it.
4. **Review and approval:** we reply to emails within one business day. We may ask for more information or ask you to send the item back for inspection.
5. **Resolution:** once approved, we send a replacement or issue a refund.

## Shipping and refunds for claims

- A prepaid return label is provided for approved warranty claims. The $5.99 return shipping fee is not deducted.
- Replacements ship free of charge, by standard shipping unless we agree otherwise.
- Refunds always go to the **original payment method** (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or PayPal), within **5-7 business days** after we receive and inspect the item. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

## Safety issues

If a product overheats, produces smoke, or has a swelling battery, **stop using it immediately**, unplug it, and keep it away from flammable materials. Then contact support right away and mention the safety issue in your message. These claims are prioritized above all others, and the warranty period will not delay our response.

## Replacement items

A replacement item carries the remaining warranty period of the original item, or 90 days from the replacement's delivery date, whichever is longer.

## Your statutory rights

This warranty is in addition to any rights you have under applicable consumer protection laws. It does not limit those rights.

## Questions

Contact support@shopflow.example with your order number and we will help you determine whether your item is covered.
