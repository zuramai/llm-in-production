# Order Cancellation Policy

*Owner: Customer Support · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This page explains when and how you can cancel a ShopFlow order, what happens to your payment, and what to do if your order has already shipped.

## Quick Summary

| Order status | Can you cancel? | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Pending | Not needed | Cancelled automatically after 48 hours with no successful payment |
| Paid | Yes, free of charge | Full refund to your original payment method |
| Shipped | No | Use a return after delivery |
| Delivered | No | Use a return (see our return policy) |

## Cancelling an Order Before It Ships

You can cancel any order free of charge until it ships.

1. Sign in and go to *Account → Orders*.
2. Select the order and click **Cancel**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

The order status changes to **cancelled**. If you cancelled after payment, you will receive a full refund.

**Tip:** Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day, so the window to cancel can be short. If you need to cancel soon after ordering, do it right away. If the Cancel option is no longer available, your order has probably already shipped.

## Pending Orders

An order is **pending** when no payment has succeeded yet, for example if your card was declined or a PayPal payment was not completed.

- A failed payment attempt is never charged.
- Your bank may show a temporary authorization hold. It normally disappears within 1-3 business days.
- Pending orders are cancelled automatically after **48 hours**. You don't need to do anything.

## Refunds for Cancelled Orders

- Refunds for cancelled orders are issued within **5-7 business days** of the cancellation.
- Refunds always go to the **original payment method** (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover or PayPal). ShopFlow cannot refund to a different card or account.
- The refund covers the full order, including shipping.

Your bank or PayPal may take additional time to show the credit on your statement.

## If Your Order Has Already Shipped

Once an order has shipped, it can't be cancelled. Instead:

1. Wait for the package to be delivered.
2. Go to *Account → Orders → Return items* within **30 days of delivery**.
3. Choose the items and a reason, then print the prepaid label.

Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Opened beauty and grocery items can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective. Return shipping is free for defective or wrong items. Otherwise, $5.99 is deducted from your refund. Returned orders are shown as **refunded** once processed.

## Changing the Delivery Address

We can change the delivery address only **before the order ships**. Contact support as soon as possible with your order number and the corrected address.

## Need Help?

If you can't cancel an order online or you aren't sure of its status, contact us:

- **Email:** support@shopflow.example
- **Chat and email hours:** Monday-Friday, 9 AM-6 PM ET; Saturday, 10 AM-4 PM ET

We reply to emails within one business day. Please include your order number so we can help faster.

If your order arrived damaged or defective, see our damaged items policy. Report it within 30 days of delivery with a photo for a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping.
