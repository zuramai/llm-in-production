# Account Security

*Owner: Customer Support · Last reviewed: 2026-10-14*

This page explains how to keep your ShopFlow account safe and what to do if you think someone else has accessed it.

## Passwords

- Use a password that is unique to ShopFlow. Don't reuse one from another site.
- Choose a long passphrase rather than a short, complex one.
- To change your password, go to *Account → Security → Change password*.
- If you forget it, use *Forgot password* on the login page. The reset email can take up to 10 minutes, so check your spam folder before requesting another.

ShopFlow staff will never ask for your password, by chat, email, or any other channel.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

We recommend turning on 2FA. Once it is enabled, signing in requires your password and a one-time code.

1. Go to *Account → Security → Two-factor authentication*.
2. Follow the prompts to set up a verification method.
3. Save your backup codes somewhere safe, separate from your password.

If you lose access to your verification method and your backup codes, contact support from the email address on the account. We will verify your identity before making any changes.

## Payment security

Card payments (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) are processed by Stripe, and PayPal payments are processed by PayPal. We accept no other payment methods, so any message asking you to pay ShopFlow by cryptocurrency, cash, or gift card is not from us.

A failed payment attempt is never charged. Your bank may show a temporary authorization hold, which disappears within 1–3 business days.

## Signs of suspicious activity

Contact us if you notice any of the following:

- Orders you didn't place, or an order with a delivery address you don't recognize
- Password reset emails you didn't request
- Changes to your account details that you didn't make
- Emails or texts that appear to come from ShopFlow but ask for your password or full card details

## If you see an order you didn't place

1. **Check the order status** under *Account → Orders*.
2. **If it hasn't shipped**, cancel it free of charge with *Account → Orders → Cancel*. Orders cancelled after payment are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
3. **If it is still pending**, it has no successful payment and will be cancelled automatically after 48 hours.
4. **If it has already shipped**, contact support as soon as possible. We can only change a delivery address before an order ships. After that, the order must be handled as a return.

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

## Locking your account

If you suspect someone else has accessed your account, contact support and ask us to lock it. We will lock it immediately. Then:

1. Reset your password using *Forgot password*.
2. Enable 2FA.
3. Review your recent orders and saved addresses.
4. Let us know about anything unfamiliar so we can investigate.

If your account is locked and you didn't request it, contact support and we will verify your identity before restoring access.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, contact support from the email address on the account. We delete personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax purposes.
